# Write like a human, letters

For cover letters, outreach, and any note one person sends another. Everything in the default card holds. On top of it:

- Open with the specific reason this reader, this posting or this problem caught your eye. Never `I am writing to express my interest`.
- Two or three short stories with numbers in them. Not a list of duties.
- Speak to the reader's situation: the size of their team, what they are standing up, who the role reports to.
- First person is expected. `I` is fine, and so is `we`. More than two sentences in a row that start with `I` is a rhythm problem, not a rule problem.
- Close in one or two plain sentences with a concrete next step. No gratitude boilerplate, no `thank you for your consideration`.
- One em dash or one semicolon per 300 words is the ceiling. Zero is better.
- No slogans. A person writing to one reader does not write lines for a poster.
- Run `/wlah check --kind letter` before it leaves.
